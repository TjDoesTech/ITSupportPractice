# <h1>IT Support - Practice</h1>

<h2>Description</h2>
This project exists to show case how I have been trying to practice IT support from home. There is a website called ServiceDesk Simulator which simulates a real time IT MSP and ticketing environment. This allows me to handle tickets and learn to solve problems that will commonly show up in an actual professional setting.
<br />



<h2>Ticket 1</h2>
Reported by: Quinn Adams (qadams@servicedesk-simulator.com)
Department: Customer Support | Location: Floor 1
Contact: x2280

Issue Description:
My desktop on the Customer Support floor won't turn on at all this morning. No lights, no fan, nothing when I press power. I have a full queue of calls waiting and I can't do anything until I have a working machine.

Troubleshooting performed by user:
- Pressed the power button several times
- Checked the wall outlet with another device (it works)
- Completely dead, no lights, no fan, no beep

Business Impact:
Customer Support agent offline; calls queueing with no one to take them.

Proposed Solution: Upon asking more clarifying questions, the computer appears to be completely dead. The deployment of a new workstation asset fixed the problem.

<h2>Ticket 3</h2>
Reported by: Kevin Park (kpark@servicedesk-simulator.com)
Department: Sales | Location: Floor 2
Contact: x5580

Issue Description:
The clock on my computer is wrong! I'm in Central time but somehow my computer is set to Eastern. The minutes look off too by a couple. All my Company Chat meetings are showing up at the wrong times and I missed one already.

Troubleshooting performed by user:
- I noticed it's set to Eastern but I'm in Central
- Restarted my computer - no change

Business Impact:
Missing meetings due to wrong timezone. File timestamps incorrect. Three more meetings scheduled this afternoon.

Proposed Solution: After talking with the client, I accessed the remote desktop tools and connected to the computer. Once I was in the computer I opened the settings and went to Time and Language. Once there I synchronized the clock to fix the issue.
